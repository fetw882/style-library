One example among many. Don't reuse its story, arc, shots, props or timings.

Story: opening of our mosquito film — "为什么同一间屋、同一个晚上，蚊子只咬你，不咬他？他一觉睡到天亮，你腿上全是包，痒得你半夜爬起来开灯找蚊子。"
Shots (single continuous shot): dust → dots gather into a house (屋 1.03) → moon gathers in the window (晚上 1.59) → white mosquito swarm flies in (蚊子 1.99) → dives to you, you pulse (你 2.49) → hovers at him, he pulses, it turns back (他 2.97) → Z's float (睡 3.97) → moon sinks (天亮 4.17) → red dots swell on your leg (包 5.19) → your dots shiver (痒 5.37) → your dots re-gather sitting up (爬起来 5.97) → lamp cone of dots (开灯 6.27) → mosquito scatters (找蚊子 6.55) → everything disperses (7.2–8.0).
